# Design Guidelines: BuildForge - Steel Building Company

## Design Approach
**Professional Steel Construction Website** - A Morton Buildings-inspired design focused exclusively on cold-formed steel buildings. The design emphasizes durability, cost-efficiency, and the advantages of steel construction over wood.

## Brand Identity
- **Company**: BuildForge
- **Focus**: Cold-formed steel buildings ONLY (not wood pole barns)
- **Value Proposition**: Durable, cost-efficient steel construction

## Core Design Elements

### Color Palette
- **Primary Brand Color**: Deep steel blue (#1E40AF - blue-800) representing strength and steel
- **Accent**: Metallic gray tones for steel emphasis
- **Neutral Base**: Clean whites and grays for professional aesthetic
- **Text**: High contrast for readability

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: Inter (professional, modern sans-serif)
- **Headings**: Bold weights (700) for hero titles, semibold (600) for sections
- **Body Text**: Regular weight (400) at text-base to text-lg
- **Overlay Text**: White text with dark overlays on hero images

### Layout System
**Spacing Primitives**: Consistent spacing using Tailwind units
- Hero sections: Full viewport height (h-screen)
- Content sections: py-16 to py-24
- Container: max-w-7xl for main content
- Grid layouts: Responsive columns

## Component Library

### Navigation Header
- Fixed/sticky header with clean background
- Logo: "BuildForge" with steel icon
- Menu: Home, See Projects, What We Build, Repairs, Renovations, 3D Studio, Contact, Admin
- Primary CTA: "Get a Quote"

### Hero Carousel
- Full-screen carousel (3-4 slides)
- Categories: Residential, Agricultural, Commercial, Equestrian
- Each slide: Steel building image, headline, description, "Learn More" CTA
- Dark gradient overlay for text contrast
- Auto-advance with manual controls

### Building Category Cards
- Clean cards with steel building photography
- Categories: Residential, Barndominiums, Shops & Garages, Horse Barns, Warehouses
- Hover effects with scale and shadow
- Clear category labels and descriptions

### Why Steel vs Wood Section
- Comparison highlighting steel advantages
- Benefits: Durability, cost-efficiency, low maintenance, fire resistance
- Branded as BuildForge expertise

### Contact/Quote Form
- Simple, professional form
- Fields: Name, Email, Phone, Project Type, Message
- Clear CTA: "Request a Quote"

### Admin Panel
- Password-protected access
- Add/Edit/Delete building categories
- Fields: Title, Description, Image URL, Tag (Residential/Agricultural/Commercial)
- Updates reflect immediately on main site

## Images
**All images MUST show steel buildings:**
- Metal siding and metal roofs
- Clean gable roof designs (no complex dormers or hip roofs)
- Steel framing (no wood posts)
- Simple, cost-effective steel construction

## Key Messaging
- Emphasize STEEL construction throughout
- Highlight advantages over wood
- Focus on durability and cost-efficiency
- Professional, trustworthy tone

This design creates a professional steel building company website that clearly differentiates from wood pole barn competitors.
