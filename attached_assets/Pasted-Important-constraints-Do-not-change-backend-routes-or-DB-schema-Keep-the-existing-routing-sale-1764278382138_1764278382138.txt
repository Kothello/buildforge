Context:

On /sales/leads/:id, clicking Save Changes successfully updates the lead in the backend and then navigates back to /sales.

On /sales, the My Leads list uses React Query useQuery to load leads and shows lead.totalPrice in the Price column.

Problem: after Save & redirect, /sales is still showing stale data until a manual browser refresh; the leads list and counts do not auto-refresh.

Desired behavior:

When a sales rep clicks Save Changes on /sales/leads/:id:

The app recalculates price with /api/pricing/calculate and sends PATCH /api/leads/:id with configuration, buildingSpecs, and the new totalPrice.

After the PATCH succeeds, the React Query cache for the leads list is invalidated/refetched.

Then the app navigates back to /sales.

When /sales renders, the My Leads list and summary tiles already show the updated lead data with the new price — no manual page refresh required.
